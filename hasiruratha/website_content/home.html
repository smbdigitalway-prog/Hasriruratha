About us

About Hasiru Ratha

The Green Chariot – Growing a Greener Bengaluru

Hasiru means green. Ratha means chariot. Our Maruti Omni, contributed by founder Dr Nagaraj Manya from his retirement funds, helps volunteers carry tools, materials and recovered tree guards to neighbourhood sites. The van is a practical symbol of a community that shows up, again and again.

Our Meaning

Hasiru Ratha means Green Chariot, symbolising our journey towards a greener and healthier Bengaluru.

Our Mission

To plant, protect and nurture trees while creating greener and healthier neighbourhoods.

Our Community

Bringing together citizens, volunteers and nature enthusiasts to take collective environmental action.

Our Commitment

Going beyond plantation by ensuring that every sapling receives care, protection and regular maintenance.
